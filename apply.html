Step 4 — Doctrinal Alignment & Network Covenant

ANCM is a Pentecostal, charismatic, apostolic network committed to the authority of Holy Scripture, the Lordship of Jesus Christ, the continuing ministry of the Holy Spirit, and the equipping of the Church for the advancement of God’s Kingdom.

Please affirm the following:

ANCM recognizes that faithful Christian ministers and churches may hold differing perspectives concerning secondary theological matters. Such differences shall not automatically prevent affiliation when the applicant remains in substantial agreement with ANCM’s foundational doctrines and mission.

Network Affiliation Covenant

By applying for affiliation with the Azusa Network of Churches and Ministries, I voluntarily affirm the following commitments:

A. Shared Faith and Mission

I recognize Jesus Christ as Lord and affirm the foundational Christian faith and mission of ANCM. I desire to participate in a network committed to strengthening ministers, equipping believers, encouraging churches, and advancing the Kingdom of God.

B. Apostolic Relationship

I understand that ANCM is an apostolic fellowship established to provide spiritual relationships, encouragement, ministry development, and appropriate ecclesiastical accountability. I enter this relationship voluntarily and with a commitment to mutual respect and Christian integrity.

C. Biblical Character and Conduct

I agree to represent my affiliation truthfully and to conduct myself consistently with biblical principles and ANCM’s applicable ethical and safeguarding standards.

D. Local Church and Ministry Autonomy

I understand that affiliation with ANCM does not surrender or transfer ownership of property, control of finances, authority over employees, selection of pastoral leadership, or internal governance of an affiliated church or ministry. Any additional oversight relationship must be established through a separate voluntary written agreement.

E. Ministerial Credentialing

I understand that affiliation with ANCM and the issuance of ministerial credentials are separate matters. Associate Minister affiliation does not constitute ministerial commissioning, licensing, or ordination. Credentialed Minister affiliation is subject to ANCM’s formal credentialing standards and approval procedures.

F. Corporate Governance

I acknowledge that network affiliation does not constitute statutory membership in the ANCM nonprofit corporation. Affiliates do not acquire corporate voting rights, ownership interests, or authority to elect corporate directors solely by virtue of their affiliation.

G. Accountability

I understand that affiliation is subject to ANCM’s governing documents and applicable policies. ANCM may review, suspend, or terminate affiliation for material violations of its established standards, subject to applicable procedures.
