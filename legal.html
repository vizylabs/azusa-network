Policy 01

Privacy Policy

The Azusa Network of Churches and Ministries (“ANCM,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of every pastor, ministry leader, church, and visitor who interacts with azusanetwork.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal and organizational information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Applicant & Member Information: When you submit a Network Affiliation Application & Covenant, register for an event, or contact us, we collect your name, preferred name, email address, telephone number, mailing address, church or ministry name, ministerial credentials, spiritual testimony, and electronic signature.
  • Payment & Billing Information: Recurring monthly affiliation payments are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processors (such as Stripe). Full credit card numbers and bank account credentials are encrypted and transmitted directly to our payment processor and are never stored in plain text on ANCM servers.
  • Local Draft Storage & Technical Data: When completing our multi-step application on /apply, your form progress is saved locally in your browser’s localStorage so you do not lose your place if you navigate away or refresh your mobile browser.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To review, process, and administer your Network Affiliation Application, ministerial credentialing, and annual good-standing renewals.
  • To process authorized monthly or annual affiliation fees and issue contribution or billing receipts.
  • To communicate regarding monthly apostolic roundtables, regional gatherings, pastoral care, and network updates.
  • To maintain official ecclesiastical records of commissioned, licensed, and ordained ministers and affiliated congregations.

3. Information Sharing & Confidentiality

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or email address to third-party marketers. Information is shared only with authorized ANCM presbytery elders, administrative staff, and trusted service providers (such as payment processors and secure email/webhook delivery infrastructure) strictly as required to operate the Network or comply with applicable law.

4. Your Privacy Rights & Data Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or unsubscribe from non-transactional network communications at any time by submitting a request through our online contact form.

Policy 02

Terms of Service & Website Use

By accessing or using azusanetwork.com, the ANCM Member Portal, or submitting a Network Affiliation Application, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.

1. Eligibility & Truthful Representation

You affirm that all personal, ministerial, and organizational information submitted to ANCM is truthful, accurate, and complete. If you submit an application on behalf of a local church or ministry organization, you represent that you have authority from that organization’s governing leadership to do so.

2. Network Affiliation vs. Ministerial Credentials

Network affiliation and ministerial credentialing are distinct:

  • Associate Minister Affiliation ($29.00/mo): Provides recognized Christian fellowship, spiritual encouragement, and leadership equipping, but does not confer ministerial commissioning, licensing, or ordination.
  • Credentialed Minister Affiliation ($59.00/mo): Provides access to ANCM’s ministerial fellowship and ecclesiastical credentialing pathway. Ministerial credentials are granted only after the applicant satisfies ANCM’s established qualifications, examination requirements, and presbytery approval. Payment of monthly affiliation fees does not guarantee commissioning, licensing, or ordination.
  • Affiliated Church & Ministry ($149.00/mo): Provides formal apostolic relationship and fellowship while preserving 100% local church autonomy over property, finances, boards, and internal governance.

3. Intellectual Property & Equipping Resources

All content on this website and inside the ANCM Member Portal—including teaching replays, church bylaw templates, leadership manuals, logos, and branding—is the property of The Azusa Network of Churches and Ministries. Active affiliates are granted a non-exclusive license to use downloadable templates and training materials within their own local church or ministry, but may not resell or redistribute them commercially.

4. Limitation of Liability

The website and member resources are provided “as is.” Church governance templates and financial stewardship checklists are provided for educational and ecclesiastical reference and do not constitute formal legal, tax, or accounting representation. Affiliated churches should consult qualified local legal or tax professionals regarding state-specific corporate or 501(c)(3) filings.

Policy 03

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

azusanetwork.com uses strictly necessary browser storage (`localStorage` and `sessionStorage`) and essential cookies to deliver a reliable, mobile-friendly experience without intrusive third-party ad tracking.

1. How We Use Cookies & Browser Storage

  • Application Progress Auto-Save (Essential): When you fill out the multi-step affiliation application on /apply, we store your non-sensitive draft text locally on your device so you never lose your work if you accidentally close a tab or switch apps on your phone.
  • Session & Portal State (Essential): Remembers your preview unlock session, active membership tier view, and cookie banner acknowledgment.
  • Payment Security Cookies (Stripe): When processing credit card or ACH payments, Stripe uses security cookies to prevent fraud and verify payment sessions.

2. Managing Your Preferences

You can choose Accept All or Essential Only in our site cookie banner, or clear your browser’s local storage and cookies at any time through your mobile or desktop browser settings.

Policy 04

Monthly Affiliation Billing, Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Monthly Affiliation Fee Schedule

  • Associate Minister: $29.00 USD per month
  • Credentialed Minister: $59.00 USD per month
  • Affiliated Church & Ministry: $149.00 USD per month

2. Recurring Billing Authorization & Automatic Renewal

By completing Step 5 of the Network Affiliation Application and checking the Recurring Billing Authorization box, you authorize ANCM and its designated payment processor (Stripe) to charge your selected credit card, debit card, or US bank account (ACH) on a recurring monthly basis until canceled.

3. How to Cancel Anytime

You may cancel your monthly affiliation at any time with zero cancellation penalties through the Billing tab inside the Member Portal or by sending a cancellation notice through our contact form. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid monthly billing cycle, and no further recurring charges will be made.

4. Refund Policy & Declined Applications

Monthly affiliation fees are non-refundable once a billing period has begun, except where required by law. However, if an initial application for affiliation is declined by the ANCM Apostolic Presbytery or withdrawn prior to initial approval, any initial affiliation fee collected at application submission will be refunded in full.

Policy 05

Ecclesiastical Governance & Local Autonomy Disclaimer

In accordance with the Network Affiliation Covenant of The Azusa Network of Churches and Ministries:

  • 100% Local Church & Ministry Autonomy: Every affiliated church, ministry, and missions organization retains full and exclusive ownership of its real property, assets, bank accounts, budgets, employment decisions, pastoral selection, and internal corporate governance.
  • No Vicarious Corporate Liability: Affiliation with ANCM is a voluntary spiritual and ecclesiastical fellowship. Neither ANCM nor any affiliated church assumes legal, financial, contractual, or tort liability for the actions, debts, employees, or operations of another affiliated church or minister.
  • No Statutory Corporate Voting Membership: Network affiliation is an ecclesiastical relationship and does not constitute statutory voting membership in the ANCM nonprofit corporation.
