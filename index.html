Our Core Values

Unlike traditional denominations that demand control over local property or boards, The Azusa Network is built on voluntary covenant relationship and three foundational commitments.

Relational Mentoring

Equipping and encouraging leaders in the church

Leadership begins with relationship, not titles. Members gain direct connection to spiritual fathers, mothers, and peer cohorts who pray for your family, strengthen your marriage, and walk with you through every season of ministry.

  • Monthly live mentoring roundtables with Apostle Dr. Scott Reece
  • Confidential pastoral counsel and marriage support
  • Peer cohorts for lead pastors, planters, and five-fold ministers

Spiritual Accountability

Providing guidance while honoring self-governance

We believe the local church is autonomous under the headship of Jesus Christ. We never claim ownership of your pulpit, property, or treasury—instead offering wise counsel, board advisory, and protective spiritual covering when invited.

  • 100% protection of your congregation’s autonomous bylaws & vision
  • Optional apostolic advisory board for healthy pastoral checks and balances
  • Recognized ecclesiastical standing, mediation, and restoration care

Effective Leadership

Empowering Spirit-empowered ministry growth

Sustained revival requires healthy wineskins. We combine a passionate pursuit of the Holy Spirit’s gifts with practical governance frameworks—helping you train elders, steward finances, and build growing congregations.

  • On-demand resource vault of church bylaws, policies, and training manuals
  • On-site apostolic ministry visits, leadership intensives, and revival services
  • Church-planting blueprints and cross-network ministry partnerships

Rooted in the Spirit of Revival. Built for Today’s Church Builders.

“In 1906, the Azusa Street movement showed what happens when hungry leaders lay aside competition and gather around the presence of Jesus. Our heart is to see pastors strengthened, marriages whole, and the fire of the Spirit passed to the next generation.”

Dr. Scott and Michelle Reece founded The Azusa Network to answer a cry heard from pastors and ministry leaders everywhere: the need for a safe, authentic spiritual family where you are known by name rather than treated as a number in an institution.

Whether you are pioneering a new church plant, pastoring an established congregation, or stewarding a five-fold itinerant or marketplace ministry, our network provides a table of encouragement, sharpening, and prayer covering.

Five-Fold Unity

Honoring apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers working together to equip the saints.

Healthy Ministry Marriages

Walking alongside both spouses in leadership so ministry growth never comes at the expense of family health.
