Our Core Values

Unlike traditional denominations that demand control over local property or boards, The Azusa Network is built on voluntary covenant relationship and three foundational commitments.

Relational Mentoring

Equipping and encouraging leaders in the church

Leadership begins with relationship, not titles. Members gain direct connection to spiritual fathers, mothers, and peer cohorts who pray for your family, strengthen your marriage, and walk with you through every season of ministry.

  • Monthly live mentoring roundtables with Apostle Dr. Scott Reece
  • Confidential pastoral counsel and marriage support
  • Peer cohorts for lead pastors, planters, and five-fold ministers

Spiritual Accountability

Providing guidance while honoring self-governance

We believe the local church is autonomous under the headship of Jesus Christ. We never claim ownership of your pulpit, property, or treasury—instead offering wise counsel, board advisory, and protective spiritual covering when invited.

  • 100% protection of your congregation’s autonomous bylaws & vision
  • Optional apostolic advisory board for healthy pastoral checks and balances
  • Recognized ecclesiastical standing, mediation, and restoration care

Effective Leadership

Empowering Spirit-empowered ministry growth

Sustained revival requires healthy wineskins. We combine a passionate pursuit of the Holy Spirit’s gifts with practical governance frameworks—helping you train elders, steward finances, and build growing congregations.

  • On-demand resource vault of church bylaws, policies, and training manuals
  • On-site apostolic ministry visits, leadership intensives, and revival services
  • Church-planting blueprints and cross-network ministry partnerships

Rooted in the Spirit of Revival. Built for Today’s Church Builders.

Section 4 — Spiritual Background and Ministry Relationship

Affiliation with another Christian organization does not automatically disqualify an applicant from participation in ANCM.

Section 5 — Doctrinal Alignment

ANCM is a Pentecostal, charismatic, apostolic network committed to the authority of Holy Scripture, the Lordship of Jesus Christ, the continuing ministry of the Holy Spirit, and the equipping of the Church for the advancement of God’s Kingdom.

Please affirm the following:

ANCM recognizes that faithful Christian ministers and churches may hold differing perspectives concerning secondary theological matters. Such differences shall not automatically prevent affiliation when the applicant remains in substantial agreement with ANCM’s foundational doctrines and mission.

Section 6 — Ministerial Credentials

Complete this section if applying for Credentialed Minister affiliation.

• Applicants seeking ANCM credentials must complete the official Ministerial Credentialing Application and applicable examination, reference, and interview requirements.

• ANCM values theological education but does not require a formal academic degree as an absolute prerequisite for licensing or ordination.

• Biblical competency, Christian character, demonstrated calling, and faithful ministerial service shall be central considerations in credentialing decisions.

Section 7 — Network Affiliation Covenant

By applying for affiliation with the Azusa Network of Churches and Ministries, I voluntarily affirm the following commitments:

A. Shared Faith and Mission

I recognize Jesus Christ as Lord and affirm the foundational Christian faith and mission of ANCM. I desire to participate in a network committed to strengthening ministers, equipping believers, encouraging churches, and advancing the Kingdom of God.

B. Apostolic Relationship

I understand that ANCM is an apostolic fellowship established to provide spiritual relationships, encouragement, ministry development, and appropriate ecclesiastical accountability. I enter this relationship voluntarily and with a commitment to mutual respect and Christian integrity.

C. Biblical Character and Conduct

I agree to represent my affiliation truthfully and to conduct myself consistently with biblical principles and ANCM’s applicable ethical and safeguarding standards.

D. Local Church and Ministry Autonomy

I understand that affiliation with ANCM does not surrender or transfer ownership of property, control of finances, authority over employees, selection of pastoral leadership, or internal governance of an affiliated church or ministry. Any additional oversight relationship must be established through a separate voluntary written agreement.

E. Ministerial Credentialing

I understand that affiliation with ANCM and the issuance of ministerial credentials are separate matters. Associate Minister affiliation does not constitute ministerial commissioning, licensing, or ordination. Credentialed Minister affiliation is subject to ANCM’s formal credentialing standards and approval procedures.

F. Corporate Governance

I acknowledge that network affiliation does not constitute statutory membership in the ANCM nonprofit corporation. Affiliates do not acquire corporate voting rights, ownership interests, or authority to elect corporate directors solely by virtue of their affiliation.

G. Accountability

I understand that affiliation is subject to ANCM’s governing documents and applicable policies. ANCM may review, suspend, or terminate affiliation for material violations of its established standards, subject to applicable procedures.

Section 8 — Monthly Affiliation and Payment Agreement

Monthly Affiliation Fees

I understand that the affiliation level I have selected carries the following monthly fee: Associate Minister: $29.00 per month · Credentialed Minister: $59.00 per month · Affiliated Church & Ministry: $149.00 per month.

Recurring Billing Authorization

By separately accepting the recurring payment authorization at checkout, I authorize ANCM or its designated payment processor to charge my selected payment method for the applicable monthly affiliation fee. The first charge will occur on the date disclosed at checkout. Subsequent charges will occur approximately monthly on the disclosed renewal date, subject to the payment processor’s calendar and billing procedures. The amount, frequency, payment method, and applicable taxes or additional charges, if any, shall be clearly disclosed before payment authorization.

Automatic Monthly Renewal & Cancellation

My paid affiliation will automatically renew each month unless canceled in accordance with ANCM’s published cancellation procedures. I may cancel my monthly affiliation at any time through the designated online cancellation process or by contacting ANCM through its published cancellation channel. Cancellation will ordinarily become effective at the end of the current paid billing period. No additional recurring affiliation charges will be made after cancellation becomes effective, except for previously authorized or outstanding amounts lawfully owed.

Refunds, Changes to Fees & Approval of Affiliation

Monthly affiliation payments are ordinarily nonrefundable after the applicable billing period begins, except where required by law or expressly provided in ANCM’s published refund policy. ANCM may modify its affiliation fees upon advance notice as required by applicable law and its published billing terms. Submission of an application or payment does not guarantee acceptance into ANCM. ANCM shall disclose how payments are handled if an application is declined or withdrawn before approval. Applicants shall not be enrolled in ongoing paid billing without the required authorization and applicable disclosures.

Ministerial Credential Renewal

I understand that annual ministerial credential renewal is separate from monthly network affiliation billing. Payment of monthly affiliation fees does not automatically renew ministerial credentials or eliminate ANCM’s doctrinal, ethical, or accountability requirements.
